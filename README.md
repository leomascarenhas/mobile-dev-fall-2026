## Getting help

The best time to ask is **in class** - bring your questions and your screen. Between sessions,
use the contact above. A specific message like *"I'm stuck at step 3, here's what I tried"* gets
you unstuck fastest. Being stuck is a normal part of learning, not something to hide.

## Handing in your work

You hand work in through **Lea (Omnivox)**, not this folder. Each week's `student-instructions.md`
says exactly what to submit. Send your **source code only** (no build or dependency folders), and
give it one last look before submitting - your work is graded exactly as you hand it in.
